WHAT IS THIS

A photocopied wall of what's actually happening near you — protests, meetings, mutual aid, shows, tenant fights, know-your-rights. Sorted by city, drilled to the neighborhood. No algorithm. No ads. No cops.

HOW IT STAYS REAL

Listings are pulled every Sunday from verified org calendars and feeds — not made up, not scraped from randos. If a feed dies, the last good issue stays up. Dead events get buried automatically.
