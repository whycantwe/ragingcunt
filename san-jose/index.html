WHAT IS THIS

A photocopied wall of what's actually happening near you — protests, meetings, mutual aid, tenant fights, know-your-rights. Sorted by city, drilled toward your block. No algorithm. No ads. No cops.

HOW IT STAYS REAL

Every listing is pulled straight from a verified org's own public calendar and refreshed weekly. Nothing here is made up or scraped from randos. If a feed goes quiet, the last good issue stays up; past events bury themselves.
